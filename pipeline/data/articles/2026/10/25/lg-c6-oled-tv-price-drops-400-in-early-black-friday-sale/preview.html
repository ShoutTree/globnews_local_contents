LG C6 OLED TV Price Drops $400 in Early Black Friday Sale

Glob News Desk · AI-assisted, human-reviewed · 2026-10-25 21:24 · 3 min read

The LG C6 OLED TV is now available for an all-time low of $999.99, marking a significant discount ahead of the November holiday shopping season.

Shoppers looking to upgrade their home entertainment setup do not need to wait for the peak of the holiday shopping season to find a deal on high-end televisions. LG has officially launched its early Black Friday sale, offering substantial discounts on its highly rated C6 OLED TV line and compatible soundbars. The promotional pricing is valid through November 1st, providing consumers with an opportunity to secure premium hardware at reduced costs well before the main sales events begin.

For those interested in the mid-range option, the 55-inch model of the C6 is currently priced at an all-time low of $999.99. This represents a savings of $400 compared to its standard retail price. Major retailers including Amazon and Best Buy are matching this aggressive pricing strategy, ensuring that customers have multiple avenues to purchase the television at the discounted rate without needing to navigate complex coupon codes or exclusive vendor restrictions.

Larger screen enthusiasts are also benefiting from the promotion. The 65-inch variant of the C6 OLED is being sold for $1,399.99, which cuts $300 off the original cost. This price point is available across Amazon, Best Buy, and directly from LG’s official storefront. For customers who choose to buy directly from the manufacturer, the purchase includes additional value-adds, specifically a complimentary Xbox Wireless Controller and a free wall mount, further enhancing the appeal of the direct-to-consumer route.

Technically, the C6 serves as a refined successor to last year’s C5 model, incorporating several incremental but meaningful upgrades. The television is powered by a faster Alpha 11 AI Gen3 processor, which LG states improves both picture rendering and audio processing capabilities. The display panel itself has been brightened to offer better visibility in well-lit rooms, addressing one of the traditional weaknesses of OLED technology while maintaining its signature contrast ratios.

Gamers will find the updated refresh rate particularly noteworthy. The C6 supports a maximum refresh rate of up to 165Hz, an increase from the 144Hz found in its predecessor. This higher frame rate contributes to smoother motion handling during fast-paced gaming sessions. The television retains four HDMI 2.1 ports and continues to support AMD FreeSync Premium and Nvidia G-Sync technologies, which help eliminate screen tearing and reduce input lag for PC and console players alike.

Beyond gaming specifications, the C6 delivers the core visual characteristics expected from OLED panels, including deep black levels and vibrant color reproduction. It supports Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos formats, allowing movies and television shows to appear more lifelike and immersive. Built-in access to major streaming platforms such as Netflix and HBO Max means users can consume content without requiring external streaming devices, while Google Cast and Apple AirPlay 2 facilitate easy content sharing from mobile phones.

Smart home integration is another key feature of the C6 ecosystem. The television supports Matter, a universal connectivity standard that allows it to pair seamlessly with various smart home devices and automation systems. This interoperability ensures that the TV can function as a central hub within a modern connected home, responding to voice commands and integrating with other IoT appliances without compatibility issues.

Despite the strong technical performance, potential buyers should be aware of recent privacy discussions surrounding LG’s smart TVs. Researchers have raised concerns regarding how the company collects and logs user data. LG has responded to these accusations by clarifying that its televisions do not continuously record conversations. Furthermore, features such as voice recognition and interest-based advertising are optional and disabled by default upon initial setup.

While LG has not addressed every aspect of the data-collection debate, similar practices are not unique to its products within the broader television industry. These privacy considerations exist alongside the C6’s impressive feature set, which remains robust for both casual viewers and avid gamers. With prices dropping significantly and key features intact, the C6 offers a compelling package for those seeking to enter the OLED market this autumn.
